# Related work and comparison with SignalWatch

Review date: 2026-10-27. Scope: eight research papers and five technical primary sources on time-series anomaly evaluation, normal-regime transfer, gradual changes, model selection and numerical preprocessing. This is a targeted literature review, not an exhaustive systematic review or a reproduced external benchmark.

## Main finding

Our observed failures resemble problems discussed in the literature: normal-distribution mismatch, unreliable confidence under shift, disagreement between ranking and alarm utility, and selection on repeatedly inspected data. We also identified a concrete numerical-conditioning weakness in our own V12 scaling. These connections support specific experiments; they do not establish the physical cause of our alarms or validate a replacement model.

SignalWatch remains a recorded-testbed research workbench. Active V5 detects 26/28 retrospective annotation segments but produces 56 false starts over 18,208 observed normal seconds, or 11.072/h. V10-V12 produce no qualifying replacement. The current goal of fewer false alarms while retaining gradual defects remains open.

## Review method and evidence boundary

We searched primary publisher/proceedings pages, author repositories and official technical guidance. Search themes covered evaluation bias, multimode normality, false-detection rates, gradual shifts, validation reuse and scaling. The [source ledger](evidence/related-work-sources-2026-10-27.json) records each URL, reviewed sections, access limits and comparison claims. Some papers were reviewed through abstracts or selected sections; those access limits remain explicit.

We compared the sources with saved V5-V12 artifacts and the existing numerical audit. We performed no new model fit, classifier inference, threshold scan, relabelling or external implementation run. The performance numbers below belong to SignalWatch. We use other work to compare methods and failure mechanisms, without placing incompatible scores in a shared leaderboard. Internal independent review means another AI Model QA Specialist; it is not external scientific peer review.

## Shared failure patterns and local strength of evidence

**Confirmed in our artifacts:** probability/ranking gains without an accepted alarm system; a held-out normal-density mismatch; the missed linear case at low false burden; numerical Pressure conditioning; and repeated use of development material. [V8](ML_V8_ABLATION_RESEARCH.md), [V11](ML_V11_TREND_BACKBONE_RESEARCH.md) and [V12](ML_V12_NORMAL_REGIME_RESEARCH.md) contain the underlying measurements.

**Plausible explanations that remain unproved:** a physical operating-mode change behind every false alert, a specific causal share from scaling or calibration, and transfer of any paper's reported gains to SKAB. We preserve benchmark label disagreements as false alerts. Resolving physical safety or label correctness would require independent process evidence.

Two reporting distinctions matter. V5's normal-alert fraction is only 1.494%, yet it starts 56 false episodes: a small time fraction can conceal frequent interruptions. The family breakdown is other 24, valve1 29, valve2 zero and normal-audit three starts; zero on one short slice does not establish a zero future rate. [Evaluation](ML_EVALUATION.md).

Our progressive retention cohort contains only the linear/exponential rotor-imbalance intervals other-6/other-9. The separate teaser has different source envelopes and an assumed-background convention; V5/V9 both miss its gradual shaft intervention. We do not merge these into a larger uniform ground-truth sample or claim broad gradual-fault competence. [V9 transfer boundary](ML_V9_BRANCH_POLICY_RESEARCH.md).

## Reporting paragraph suitable for the project report

We conducted a targeted review of related work on time-series anomaly evaluation, multimode normality, sequential false detections and model-selection bias. We compared the sources with SignalWatch's preserved experiments and documented overlapping failure patterns, including normal-distribution mismatch and disagreement between probability metrics and alarm utility. V12 also exposed poor numerical conditioning under a zero-IQR Pressure scale. These comparisons are qualitative: we did not reproduce external algorithms in a common benchmark, establish physical causes or demonstrate superiority. Active V5 remains a known-state classifier/normal-predictor research baseline, with unresolved false-alert and gradual-defect limitations.

## Priorities justified by this review

1. **Numerical preflight before another learner study.** Distinguish a constant channel from zero IQR with rare valid levels. Compare training-only, scale-aware alternatives, inspect transformed ranges and check centered Gaussian arithmetic. Preserve frozen V12; a corrected run needs its own protocol. K2's failure despite a stable fixed-parameter audit shows that K4 conditioning is not the only open issue.
2. **Test invariance to normal operating levels.** A bounded learned-feature ablation could compare absolute levels with causal within-segment references and relative changes. The existing two-observation reference can contain an anomaly; unknown-stream cadence startup is also unverified. Reference subtraction or adaptation can remove useful gradual-fault evidence, so invariance must be tested against retained defects and delay. This is a hypothesis, not an implemented fix or permission to normalize a whole test recording.
3. **Evaluate gradual detection and false burden together.** Broaden compatible real recordings and report onset delay, missed intervals, exposure and per-record/family burden. Artificial drifts can serve as labelled stress tests, separately from real-fault evidence. Optional EWMA/CUSUM comparisons must preserve their latency and false-alarm costs and the project's genuine-ML objective.
4. **Reserve an untouched confirmation set.** Freeze the next hypothesis and comparator before evaluation. Keep the old audit and inspected teaser labelled retrospective. Report uncertainty at the recording level with dependence/selection limits; no significance claim follows from 28 gap-split segments alone.

These are proposals for future agreed work. This review adds no model, new decision rule, numerical correction or quality improvement claim.

## Positioning and verification

The project's current contribution is an auditable engineering study: genuine trained components, explicit episode/exposure definitions, causal replay under declared preloaded cadence, baseline wins and preserved negative results. We claim neither a new learning algorithm, SOTA performance nor industrial readiness. Grouped fitting protects against row leakage; it does not certify new-machine or prospective transfer.
